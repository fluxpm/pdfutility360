Privacy Policy

PDF Utility 360 · Last updated October 21, 2026

1. Who we are and what this covers

This Privacy Policy explains how PDF Utility 360 (the “App”), provided by Flux Project Management Inc. (“we”, “us”), handles information. It applies to the App on Android devices. By installing or using the App you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please uninstall the App.

2. Short version

The App works entirely on your device. We do not collect, receive, store, sell, share or transmit your personal information or your files. The App does not request internet access, has no accounts, and contains no advertising or analytics.

3. Information we collect

None. We do not collect personal data, device identifiers, location, contacts, usage analytics, crash reports or advertising IDs.

4. Your files

The App opens a PDF or image only when you choose it using Android’s system file picker. Files are read and processed on your device (in memory and, for large saves, in a temporary cache file that is deleted after saving). The App writes a result only to the location you choose. Your files, and the results, never leave your device through the App, and we never receive a copy.

5. Information stored on your device

The App stores a few preferences locally (for example theme, preview/output quality and whether you have seen the introduction). These stay on your device and can be removed by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling it.

6. Permissions

The App does not require special permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location or general storage. File access is limited to files you explicitly select or locations you explicitly choose when saving.

7. Third parties

The App does not send data to any third party. It bundles open-source software that runs locally on your device (PDF.js and pdf-lib). Opening the Play Store from the “Rate” button, or the share sheet from the “Share” button, is handled by Android and the apps you choose; those services have their own privacy policies.

8. Children

The App is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your region). Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we hold no information about children.

9. Security

Because processing happens on your device, the security of your files depends on your device’s security. Keep your device updated and protected with a screen lock. Always review a redacted, edited or compressed file before sharing it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or California’s CCPA/CPRA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal data. We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to retrieve or delete. You can contact us with any privacy question at the address below.
